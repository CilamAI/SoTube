Troubleshooting & FAQ
Why did a video download fail or time out?
Downloads can stall due to YouTube bot verification, regional restrictions, or unstable network. Confirm your internet connection, verify the link is public, and retry using 720p or 480p format.
How do I convert YouTube videos to MP3?
Click the format dropdown next to the search bar and select "MP3 Audio". SoTube will extract the audio track and encode it into high quality 320kbps MP3 automatically.
How do I use the Lossless Video Trimmer?
Click the Trim tab in the sidebar, select a video file, and set start and end points using the interactive playback slider. Click "Start Trimming" to cut without quality loss.
Where are downloaded files stored?
Files are saved in your Windows Downloads folder by default. You can customize the download destination anytime in Settings.
