Frequently Asked Questions
Why did my download or search request fail?
Download issues typically occur due to temporary network blocks or bot protection. Verify your internet connection, confirm the link is accessible in a web browser, and try selecting "720p" or "480p" format.
How do I download audio only as MP3?
Click the format selector next to the search input and choose "MP3 Audio". SoTube will extract the audio stream and convert it to high-bitrate MP3 automatically.
How does the Lossless Trimmer work?
Navigate to the Trim tab, choose a video file, and set start and end points using the interactive playback slider. Click "Start Trimming" to cut with zero re-encoding loss.
Where are my downloads saved?
By default, files are saved in your Windows Downloads folder. You can change this anytime under Settings > Download Folder.
